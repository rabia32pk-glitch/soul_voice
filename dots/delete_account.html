Account Deletion

Delete Your Soul Voice Account

Soul Voice by Innovex Technologies | User Data & Privacy

We respect your privacy and provide full control over your personal data. If you wish to permanently delete your Soul Voice account and remove all personal information associated with it, you can do so using either of the two methods below:

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account instantly from within the Soul Voice application:

  1. Open the Soul Voice app on your device.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (the rightmost icon on the bottom navigation bar).
  3. Scroll to the bottom of your Profile screen.
  4. Tap the red Delete Account button.
  5. Tap Delete on the confirmation popup to confirm.

Result: Your account is instantly erased from Google Firebase Authentication, your profile document is deleted from Cloud Firestore, your local session is cleared, and you are redirected to the login screen.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can request manual account deletion by emailing our support team:

Send Email To: innovexa.technologies01@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - Soul Voice

Required Details:

  • The email address registered with your Soul Voice account.

Processing Time: Email requests are processed manually within 24 to 48 hours (maximum 7 business days).

What Data Is Permanently Deleted?

  • Firebase Authentication Record: Your account UID, registered email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Cloud Database Data: Your user document in Google Cloud Firestore (users/{uid}) containing your display name.
  • Local Device Storage: Cached profile image, saved favorite quotes, and theme preferences stored on your device.

⚠️ Important Note

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your saved quotes and preferences cannot be recovered. You may register a new account at any time if you wish to use Soul Voice again.
