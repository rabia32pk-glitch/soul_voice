Soul Voice — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 • Innovex Technologies
Summary: Soul Voice is an offline-friendly inspirational quotes application. We respect your privacy: we do not collect personal identifying information, do not require user accounts, and never sell or share data with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Soul Voice is designed to work without collecting personal user data:

  • No User Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, phone number, or email address.
  • Local Preferences Only: Your app settings (such as Dark/Light theme mode and bookmarked favorite quotes) are stored locally on your device storage.

2. How We Use Information

Because Soul Voice does not collect personal information, your usage is private:

  • To display motivational, wisdom, and inspirational quotes.
  • To preserve your favorited quotes locally on your device for offline reading.
  • To allow you to switch themes according to your preference.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising

We believe in a clean, peaceful experience:

  • We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade any user information.
  • We DO NOT share user data with third-party tracking or advertising networks.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

All bookmarked quotes and theme preferences exist exclusively inside your device's local application storage. You can delete all saved preferences at any time simply by clearing the application cache/data or uninstalling Soul Voice from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Soul Voice provides universally positive, inspirational quotes suitable for all audiences. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.
