Privacy Policy

Soul Voice Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026 | Developer: Innovex Technologies

Welcome to Soul Voice. This application is created and maintained by Innovex Technologies ("we", "our", or "us"). Soul Voice is designed to provide inspiring quotes, reflections, Islamic wisdom, and motivational thoughts. We respect your privacy and believe in complete transparency regarding the minimal data our app interacts with.

1. Information We Collect

We only process data necessary for user authentication, saving your personal quote preferences, and delivering app functionality:

  • Account Credentials: When you create an account or sign in using Email/Password or Google Sign-In, we store your email address, display name, and a unique authentication ID (UID) via Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.
  • Profile Photo: When you choose to set or update your profile photo, the image file is saved solely in your device's local application directory. We do not upload your photos to external public servers.
  • Favorites & Bookmarks: Quotes that you add to your Favorites are saved locally on your device (via SharedPreferences) so you can read and access them offline.
  • App Preferences: Your selected Theme (Dark or Light mode) and Notification preferences (daily quotes, new quotes, reminders) are stored locally on your device.
  • Network Status: We check active internet connectivity (using standard network checks) solely to fetch quotes from the Quote API when online.

🛡️ What We Do NOT Collect

Soul Voice does NOT collect GPS location, financial or payment information, contacts, biometric data, background device tracking, or advertising identifiers. Soul Voice is 100% free with no paid transactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide the core Soul Voice user experience:

  • To authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • To display your personalized display name and avatar on the Profile page.
  • To display your saved favorite quotes and personal settings.
  • To deliver local inspirational notifications if enabled by you.

3. Third-Party Services

Soul Voice integrates reliable and trusted infrastructure providers to deliver its services:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Handles secure sign-in, authentication tokens, and password reset emails.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Securely stores user profile display names.
  • Google Sign-In: Facilitates standard Google OAuth login.
  • Quote API: Provides access to categorized motivational and spiritual quote feeds.

4. Data Storage and Security

All network communication between Soul Voice and Firebase services is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. User credentials and databases are hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure adhering to ISO 27001 and SOC 2 security compliance standards.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have total control over your personal data at all times:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all data directly inside the app by going to Profile > Delete Account. This immediately removes your Firebase Auth account, Firestore document, and local cache.
  • Email Request: If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at innovexa.technologies01@gmail.com.

For complete details, please visit our Account Deletion Page.

6. Children's Privacy

Soul Voice contains general inspirational and educational content suitable for everyone. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory standards. The updated date will always be visible at the top of this page.
